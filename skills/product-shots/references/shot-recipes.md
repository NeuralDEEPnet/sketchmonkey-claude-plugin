# Shot recipes

Each recipe gives the parts of a render prompt that follow the product fingerprint. Always end prompts with: "Photorealistic, true-to-material, no text, no logos, no watermark." Send them with `autoEnhance: false`.

## Core shots

### Hero
- **Use for:** website header, product page lead image, pitch slides.
- **View:** three-quarter front, camera at product height or slightly above.
- **Set:** seamless paper sweep in a soft neutral (warm grey, sand, pale sage) chosen to flatter the product colours.
- **Light:** large soft key light from one side, gentle rim light to separate the silhouette, soft contact shadow.
- **Lens:** 85mm, f/8, crisp focus across the product.
- **Composition:** product slightly off-centre, clear space on one side for a headline.
- **Ratio:** `4:3`, or `16:9` for website banners.
- **Lens preset:** `kai` for premium goods, `ren` for technical products.

### Clean packshot (marketplace)
- **Use for:** Amazon, Etsy, Shopify and catalogue listings.
- **View:** straight-on or slight three-quarter, the angle buyers expect.
- **Set:** pure white background (#FFFFFF), no props.
- **Light:** even, shadow-free high-key softbox lighting, a subtle soft shadow under the product only.
- **Lens:** 100mm, f/11, everything sharp.
- **Composition:** product centred, filling about 85% of the frame.
- **Ratio:** `1:1`.
- **Lens preset:** `kai`.
- **Note:** marketplaces often require a pure white background and no added text or props in the main listing image; this recipe follows that.

### Lifestyle scene
- **Use for:** product pages, social posts, ads.
- **View:** natural eye-level or slightly elevated, as if photographed in use or on display.
- **Set:** a real setting that fits the buyer. Pick one from the setting ideas below and describe two or three props at most.
- **Light:** natural window light or golden-hour sun, soft shadows.
- **Lens:** 50mm, f/2.8, product sharp, background gently soft.
- **Composition:** product clearly the subject, filling about a third of the frame.
- **Ratio:** `3:2`, or `3:4` for social feeds.
- **Lens preset:** `maya` for homes and wellness, `kai` for premium, `zola` for moody luxury.

## More shots

### Detail close-up
- **Use for:** showing quality, materials, controls and texture.
- **View:** macro on the most distinctive detail (a knurled dial, stitching, glaze texture, a hinge).
- **Light:** low raking light that reveals surface texture.
- **Lens:** 100mm macro, shallow depth of field, focus on the detail.
- **Ratio:** `1:1` or `4:3`.

### Colourway line-up
- **Use for:** showing every finish at once.
- **View:** three to five identical units in a neat row or gentle arc, same angle.
- **Set:** seamless neutral sweep.
- **Prompt tip:** list each unit's colour in order with name and hex, and add "identical shape and size, only the colour differs".
- **Ratio:** `16:9` or `3:2`.

### Flat lay
- **Use for:** kits, accessories, packaging, unboxing.
- **View:** straight top-down, 90 degrees.
- **Set:** textured surface (linen, terrazzo, light oak) with a few related items arranged on a loose grid.
- **Light:** soft, even daylight.
- **Ratio:** `1:1` or `3:4`.

### Scale in context
- **Use for:** answering "how big is it?".
- **Set:** next to familiar everyday objects of known size (a coffee cup, a paperback, a smartphone, a pen) on a desk or counter.
- **Prompt tip:** state the real size ("about 30 cm tall") and the reference objects.
- **Ratio:** `4:3`.

### Social vertical
- **Use for:** Stories, Reels and TikTok covers.
- **View:** hero angle, product in the lower-middle third.
- **Composition:** keep the top quarter free for text and keep important detail out of the bottom fifth, where app controls sit.
- **Ratio:** `9:16`.

### Dramatic key visual
- **Use for:** launch campaigns and posters.
- **Set:** dark backdrop (charcoal, deep navy) or a sculptural plinth.
- **Light:** single strong key light with crisp rim highlights and deep shadows, optional light haze.
- **Composition:** generous negative space for a headline.
- **Ratio:** `3:4` for posters, `16:9` for banners.
- **Lens preset:** `zola`, or `kai` for a cleaner premium look.

### Seasonal or occasion scene
- **Use for:** holiday campaigns, gifting, launches.
- **Set:** subtle seasonal cues (winter light and wool throws, spring blossoms, a gift ribbon), never overpowering the product.
- **Ratio:** `3:2` or `3:4`.

## Setting ideas by product type

| Product type | Settings that sell |
|---|---|
| Kitchen and tableware | Sunlit kitchen counter in light stone, breakfast table with linen, open shelving |
| Lighting and decor | Calm living room at dusk, bedside table, reading nook with a lounge chair |
| Tech and audio | Minimal desk setup with a laptop and plant, studio shelf, bedside at night |
| Beauty and personal care | Marble bathroom shelf, vanity with soft morning light, wet stone with water droplets |
| Outdoor and sport | Trailhead at golden hour, wooden deck, gym bench with a towel |
| Fashion accessories | Concrete plinth, dressing table, café table by a window |
| Kids and pets | Bright playroom floor with soft rugs, sunny garden lawn |
| Furniture | Airy room with white walls and an oak floor, gallery-like space |

## Lighting cheat sheet

| Mood | Lighting words |
|---|---|
| Clean and premium | Large softbox key, white bounce fill, crisp rim light |
| Warm and homely | Low golden-hour window light, warm 2700K practical lamps |
| Fresh and natural | Overcast daylight, soft shadows, airy |
| Dramatic | Single hard key from the side, deep shadows, light haze |
| Technical | Even top light, gradient reflections, precise specular highlights |
