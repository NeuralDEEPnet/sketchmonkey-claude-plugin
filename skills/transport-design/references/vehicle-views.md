# Vehicle view recipes

Each recipe gives the camera, setting and lighting part of the prompt. Put the full vehicle package and the design language in front of it, and end with "Photorealistic transport design render, no text, no badges, no logos, no licence plate characters."

## Studio lighting that makes surfaces read

- **Vehicle studio:** "large overhead softbox, long horizontal light strips reflected along the body side, dark seamless floor with a soft reflection, neutral grey cyclorama." Best for side and three-quarter views; it shows feature lines and surface tension.
- **Daylight exterior:** "overcast soft daylight on a clean concrete apron, gentle sky reflections." Honest and calm.
- **Golden hour:** "low warm sun from the rear three-quarter, long shadows, rim light on the shoulder line." For hero and launch images.
- **Night:** "dark wet asphalt, lamp signature lit, cool ambient fill." Shows lamp graphics.

## Cars, vans, buses and trucks

| View | Camera part of the prompt | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Side profile | "Pure side elevation, camera at mid-wheel height, 135 mm lens equivalent, no perspective distortion, both wheels the same size" | `16:9` |
| Front three-quarter | "Front three-quarter view from about 30° off the centre line, camera at headlamp height, 50 mm lens, full vehicle in frame" | `16:9` |
| Rear three-quarter | "Rear three-quarter view from about 30° off the centre line, camera at tail-lamp height, 50 mm lens" | `16:9` |
| Front elevation | "Straight front elevation, camera at headlamp height, long lens, symmetrical" | `4:3` |
| Top / plan | "Overhead plan view looking straight down, orthographic feel" | `16:9` |
| Interior | "Interior from behind the driver's shoulder toward the instrument panel, 24 mm lens, soft daylight through the glass" or "cabin overview from the rear corner" | `16:9` |
| Detail | "Close-up of the [lamp signature / wheel / door handle], macro lens, shallow depth of field" | `4:3` |

## Motorcycles, scooters and bicycles

- Side profile is the hero; use "pure side view, camera at hub height, long lens".
- Front three-quarter from slightly low ("camera at knee height") to give presence.
- Detail: tank and seat junction, headlamp, drivetrain or battery integration.
- Ratios: `16:9` or `3:2` for side views, `4:3` for three-quarters, `3:4` for social.

## Trains and trams

- Nose three-quarter on a platform: "front three-quarter of the cab end at platform level, 35 mm lens, station canopy softly out of focus."
- Side elevation for livery: "long side elevation of one car, orthographic feel, platform-level camera."
- Interior: "saloon view down the aisle from one end, seats both sides, daylight through wide windows."
- Ratios: `16:9` throughout; `3:2` for interior.

## Aircraft, eVTOLs and helicopters

- Exterior three-quarter on a clean apron or in flight against soft sky: "front three-quarter, slightly above wing level, 70 mm lens."
- Cabin: "cabin view down the aisle from the front monument, seats in rows, window light."
- Keep rotor, propeller and wing counts in the package and repeat them; image models often add or drop them.

## Boats and yachts

- Profile at sea: "pure side profile on calm water, low camera just above the waterline, horizon level."
- Three-quarter running shot: "front three-quarter from slightly above, light wake, bright sea light."
- Deck plan: "overhead view of the deck layout, orthographic feel."

## Mobility pods and people movers

- Treat them like small buses: side profile, front three-quarter in a pedestrian street, interior seating overview.
- Show a doorway at human height to make scale clear without adding people.
