# Space planning: sizes, sightlines and plain words

Use these numbers as starting points in the space package, and say they are typical values to confirm on site.

## Typical sizes

| Thing | Typical size |
|---|---|
| Small trade fair stand | 3 × 3 m, walls 2.5 m high |
| Medium stand | 6 × 3 m or 6 × 6 m |
| Pop-up shop or kiosk | 6 to 15 m² |
| Counter or standing table | 90 to 110 cm high, 60 cm deep |
| Seated table | 72 to 75 cm high |
| Plinth for a hand-sized product | 90 to 110 cm high, 30 to 40 cm square |
| Low platform for a large product (bike, armchair) | 10 to 20 cm high |
| Shelf spacing for small products | 30 to 40 cm |
| Best shelf zone for picking up | 80 to 150 cm above the floor |
| Eye level, standing | about 1.6 m |
| Aisle between fixtures | at least 90 cm; 120 cm where two people pass |
| Wheelchair turning circle | 150 cm |
| Door | about 90 cm wide, 2.1 m high |

## Layout rules of thumb

- **One idea from the aisle.** A visitor decides in about three seconds. Put one hero product or one bold surface where it is seen first.
- **Open the corner.** On a stand with two open sides, keep the corner free; it is the entrance.
- **Attract, try, buy.** Hero at the front, hands-on area in the middle, counter and storage at the back or side.
- **Group in odd numbers.** Three or five products per plinth or shelf read better than two or four.
- **Leave air.** Half-empty shelves look premium; full shelves look like a stockroom.
- **Light the product, not the floor.** Warm spots (about 2700 to 3000 K) on the products, softer light elsewhere.
- **Hide the practical things.** Name storage, bins and cables in the package ("lockable storage inside the counter") so the render leaves room for them.

## Plain words for trade terms

| Term | In plain words |
|---|---|
| Footprint | The floor area the space takes up |
| Plinth | A stand that lifts one product up |
| Gondola | A free-standing shelf unit you can walk around |
| Fascia | The board above a stand or shop front, where the name usually goes |
| Sightline | What you can see from where you stand |
| Circulation | The route people take through the space |
| Shop-in-shop | A branded corner inside a bigger store |
| Back wall | The wall visitors face when they walk in |
| Reveal or shadow gap | A thin dark gap between two surfaces, which makes them look crisp |
| Limewash | A matte, slightly cloudy mineral paint |
| Terrazzo | Polished floor with small stone chips |
