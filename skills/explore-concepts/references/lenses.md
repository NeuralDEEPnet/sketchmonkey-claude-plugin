# Lens profiles

Use these to describe each lens in plain words, to mix lenses, and to write render prompts that capture a lens without changing what the person asked for. Lens materials are suggestions; the person's own materials always come first.

## Ren · precise and engineered
- **Feeling:** calm, trustworthy, timeless; functional modernism where every line has a reason.
- **Form language:** strict geometry, consistent radii, crisp parting lines and chamfers, nothing decorative.
- **Typical materials:** bead-blasted anodised aluminium, matte powder-coated steel, fine-textured polymer.
- **Palette mood:** anthracite, off-white and slate greys with one signal accent (orange, yellow or green).
- **Lighting:** soft overhead studio light with clean specular lines along edges.
- **Best for:** electronics, audio, tools, kitchen appliances, instruments.
- **Prompt phrases:** "precise geometric form", "crisp chamfered edges", "honest materials", "single accent colour".

## Maya · friendly and ergonomic
- **Feeling:** approachable, warm, effortless to use.
- **Form language:** soft continuous curves, generous radii, comfortable grip zones, clear and simple controls.
- **Typical materials:** soft-touch recycled polymers, silicone overmoulds, frosted translucent plastics, light woods.
- **Palette mood:** calm navies, fog greys and fresh blues or warm neutrals with a gentle accent.
- **Lighting:** wrap-around soft light with a gentle rim glow.
- **Best for:** handhelds, wearables, smart home, wellness and kids' products.
- **Prompt phrases:** "soft rounded form", "inviting tactile surfaces", "human-scaled controls".

## Kai · polished and premium
- **Feeling:** aspirational and retail-ready; the flagship on the shelf.
- **Form language:** perfect symmetry, confident proportions, jewellery-like details such as knurling and fine bevels.
- **Typical materials:** mirror-polished metals, deep gloss lacquer, sapphire-like glass, foil accents.
- **Palette mood:** black and white with champagne, gold or titanium accents.
- **Lighting:** rim-lit infinity cove with long gradient reflections.
- **Best for:** beauty, fragrance, watches, premium packaging, flagship devices.
- **Prompt phrases:** "premium retail presentation", "flawless reflections", "precise metallic accents".

## Zola · dramatic and sculptural
- **Feeling:** bold, editorial, a statement object.
- **Form language:** monolithic volumes, strong contrasts of mass and void, architectural cuts.
- **Typical materials:** cast bronze, smoked glass, honed stone, blackened metal, raw textures.
- **Palette mood:** obsidian, stone and stucco with a single deep accent such as crimson.
- **Lighting:** single theatrical key light, deep shadows, light haze.
- **Best for:** luxury accessories, statement furniture, art objects, fashion.
- **Prompt phrases:** "sculptural monolithic form", "chiaroscuro lighting", "gallery presentation".

## Nova · futuristic and organic
- **Feeling:** experimental and from the near future.
- **Form language:** organic, biomimetic shapes; lattices, perforations and flowing transitions.
- **Typical materials:** 3D-printed lattices, translucent gradient resins, iridescent films, woven composites.
- **Palette mood:** deep black and indigo with luminous mint, cyan or violet accents.
- **Lighting:** dual-tone glow, internal light pipes, soft volumetric light.
- **Best for:** concept gear, footwear, gaming, sci-fi looks, trade-show showpieces.
- **Prompt phrases:** "biomimetic lattice structure", "flowing organic form", "luminous accents".

## Mixing lenses

Name what you take from each lens: "Ren's precise geometry and chamfered edges with Maya's soft-touch grip zones and warm neutral palette." Use the lens whose overall mood should dominate as the `preset`.

## Picking a lens for someone who isn't sure

| "Bold", "artistic", "statement", "editorial" | Zola |
