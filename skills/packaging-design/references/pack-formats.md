# Pack formats and when to use them

Pick the format from the product's weight and fragility, the channel and the budget. Costs are relative; confirm real prices with a supplier.

## Boxes and cartons

| Format | In plain words | Good for | Cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tuck-end folding carton | Thin printed board box with tuck-in flaps at both ends | Light products on a shelf: cosmetics, tea, small electronics | Low |
| Crash-lock carton | Folding carton with a base that snaps flat and strong | Heavier jars and bottles | Low to medium |
| Sleeve and tray | A tray that slides out of a printed sleeve | Chocolate, soap, gift items; a nice reveal | Medium |
| Rigid two-piece box | Thick wrapped board, lift-off lid | Premium gifts, candles, watches, phones | High |
| Rigid magnetic box | Thick board with a hinged lid and hidden magnet | Gift sets, keepsakes | High |
| Mailer box | Corrugated box that folds shut without tape | Online orders and subscriptions; the outside is the first impression | Low to medium |
| Shipping carton with printed sleeve or sticker | Plain brown box, branded by one printed element | Small brands starting out | Lowest |

## Containers

| Format | In plain words | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Glass jar or bottle with label | Stock container, custom label and closure | Food, sauces, candles, skincare |
| Tin | Seamed or deep-drawn metal with a slip or hinged lid | Tea, sweets, balms; reusable |
| Stand-up pouch | Flexible bag with a base gusset and zip | Coffee, snacks, refills |
| Tube | Squeezable, printed all round | Creams, pastes |
| Paper tube | Rolled board cylinder with a cap | Tea, candles, posters, bottles |

## Inside the pack

| Insert | In plain words | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| Card insert | Folded board that holds the product in place | Light items, low cost |
| Moulded pulp tray | Shaped recycled-paper tray | Fragile items, an honest natural look |
| Foam insert | Cut foam with a product-shaped pocket | Precision items; less easy to recycle |
| Tissue and seal sticker | Wrapped product with a small branded sticker | Unboxing moment at low cost |
| Paper shred or wood wool | Loose natural fill | Gift sets and hampers |

## Sizing rules of thumb

- Outer size = product size + 3 to 5 mm clearance per side for a snug carton, or + 10 to 20 mm per side with an insert.
- Keep empty space small. An oversized box costs more to ship and reads as wasteful.
- Shelf packs: the front face should carry the product's name zone in its upper two-thirds, where it stays visible behind a shelf lip.
- Online packs: the pack is seen as a thumbnail first, so one strong shape or colour matters more than detail.

## Print and finish, in plain words

| Term | In plain words |
|---|---|
| Uncoated board | Matte, slightly rough paper feel; colours look softer |
| Coated board | Smooth surface; colours look sharp and rich |
| Kraft | Natural brown, unbleached board |
| Soft-touch lamination | Velvety matte film; premium but harder to recycle |
| Foil stamping | Metallic or coloured foil pressed into the surface |
| Emboss / deboss | A raised or pressed-in shape you can feel; "blind" means without ink |
| Spot gloss | A shiny varnish on selected areas of a matte surface |
| One-colour print | A single ink; the cheapest and often the calmest look |
| Dieline | The flat cutting and folding template the printer works from |
| gsm | Paper weight; 300 to 400 gsm is typical for folding cartons |
