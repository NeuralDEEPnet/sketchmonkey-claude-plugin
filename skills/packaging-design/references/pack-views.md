# Pack view recipes

Each recipe gives the setting, light and camera part of the prompt. Put the full pack specification and the design language in front of it, and end with "Photorealistic packaging photograph, true-to-material, blank label areas, no readable text, no logos, no barcodes."

## Light that makes board and print read

- **Soft studio:** "large soft light from the upper left, gentle shadow to the right, seamless warm-white background." The default; shows shape and colour honestly.
- **Raking light:** "low side light skimming the surface." Shows emboss, foil and paper texture; use it for the detail view.
- **Daylight table:** "soft window daylight on a pale oak table, natural shadows." For kraft, uncoated and natural packs.
- **Dark and premium:** "dark seamless background, narrow soft light from above, subtle reflection below." For rigid gift boxes and foil.

## The standard set

| View | Setting and camera part of the prompt | Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Front | "Straight-on packshot of the front face, camera level with the centre of the pack, 85 mm lens, no perspective distortion, soft studio light" | `4:3` |
| Three-quarter | "Three-quarter view from slightly above showing the front, one side and the top, 70 mm lens, soft shadow" | `4:3` |
| Open | "The pack open, lid resting beside it, the product seated in its insert, seen from 45° above, 50 mm lens" | `4:3` |
| In context, shelf | "On a retail shelf at eye level between plain, unbranded neutral packs, slightly off-centre, shallow depth of field" | `16:9` |
| In context, delivery | "On a doormat in soft daylight, seen from standing height" or "on a desk next to a laptop and a cup, seen from above" | `16:9` |
| Detail | "Macro close-up of the [emboss / foil mark / closure / paper texture], raking light, shallow depth of field" | `4:3` |
| Flat lay | "Top-down flat lay of the open pack, the product, the insert and the tissue laid out neatly, even soft light" | `1:1` |
| Range | "Three packs of the same design in [colour 1], [colour 2] and [colour 3] side by side, straight-on, evenly spaced" | `16:9` |

## By format

- **Folding cartons:** show a crisp fold and a visible tuck flap edge; add "thin board, sharp creases".
- **Rigid boxes:** add "thick wrapped board, square edges, visible lid depth"; the open view should show the lid's inside colour.
- **Mailer boxes:** show both closed and open; the inside print is the reveal, so name the inside colour or pattern.
- **Jars and bottles:** give the label height as a share of the container ("label covers the middle third") and the closure material and colour.
- **Pouches:** add "filled and standing, soft creases at the gusset"; a flat pouch looks empty.
- **Tins:** name the seam and lid type; metal needs "soft reflections, no harsh highlights".

## Listing and social formats

- `1:1` marketplace image: the three-quarter recipe on a pure white background, with "square 1:1 format" in the prompt.
- `9:16` story: the open view with "vertical framing, hands-free, pack centred in the lower two-thirds".
- `3:4` feed: the flat lay or the detail.
