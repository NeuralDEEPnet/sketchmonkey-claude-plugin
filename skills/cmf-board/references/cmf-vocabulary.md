# CMF vocabulary

Precise words make better boards and better renders. Name each material with its process, finish and colour, for example "bead-blasted anodised aluminium, warm silver" rather than "metal".

## Materials

| Family | Materials | Character |
|---|---|---|
| Metals | Aluminium (6000-series), stainless steel, zinc alloy, brass, copper, titanium, magnesium | Cool, precise, durable, premium |
| Hard plastics | ABS, PC/ABS, polycarbonate, PBT, recycled PET, bio-based PLA | Light, mouldable, cost-efficient |
| Soft materials | Silicone, TPU, TPE overmould, soft-touch coating | Grippy, warm, protective |
| Woods | Oak, walnut, ash, beech, bamboo, cork | Warm, natural, ages well |
| Ceramics and glass | Stoneware, porcelain, borosilicate glass, frosted glass | Pure, tactile, food-safe feel |
| Textiles and leathers | Knit, felt, canvas, recycled polyester mesh, vegetable-tanned leather, vegan leather | Soft, acoustic, homely |
| Stone and composites | Marble, terrazzo, concrete, carbon fibre, recycled composite boards | Heavy, architectural, technical |

## Finishes

| Finish | Looks and feels | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| Bead-blasted | Fine even satin, diffuse sheen | Hides fingerprints well |
| Brushed | Directional linear grain | Shows scratches less on the grain |
| Anodised (clear or dyed) | Durable coloured metal surface | Colour can vary slightly by batch |
| Polished or mirror | High gloss, strong reflections | Shows fingerprints and scratches |
| PVD coating | Deep metallic colours (black, gold, bronze) | Very hard-wearing |
| Powder coat | Even, slightly textured colour | Robust, wide colour range |
| Soft-touch paint | Velvety, warm | Can wear on high-touch areas over years |
| High-gloss lacquer | Liquid, piano-like | Premium, needs careful handling |
| Mould texture (e.g. fine spark) | Uniform matte grain on plastics | Cheap to apply in the tool |
| Knurling | Crisp diamond or straight grip pattern | Signals precision on dials and caps |
| Glaze (matte, satin, reactive) | Ceramic depth and variation | Reactive glazes vary piece to piece |
| Oiled or waxed wood | Natural, open grain | Needs occasional care |

## Colour roles

- **Hero:** the main body colour, about 60–70% of the visible surface.
- **Secondary:** supporting parts such as base, back or trim, about 20–30%.
- **Accent:** one small, deliberate detail (a button, a ring, a tab) under 10%.
- **Neutral:** cables, feet, seals and other parts that should disappear.

Give each colour a memorable name and an approximate hex value. Note that hex values are screen approximations; final colours need physical samples.

## Practical notes worth adding

- Fingerprints: gloss and dark colours show them most.
- Wear: soft-touch coatings and dyed edges can wear on high-contact areas.
- Cleaning: open-grain woods and textiles need care in kitchens and bathrooms.
- Sustainability: recycled aluminium, recycled plastics, mono-material designs and removable fasteners make recycling easier.
- Cost tier: polishing, PVD and multi-material assemblies cost more than single-colour moulded parts.
