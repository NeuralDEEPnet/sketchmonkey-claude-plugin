Before we show the client, critique our Bluetooth speaker concept. It's for teenagers' bedrooms and should feel playful but not cheap. The concept: a 20 cm sphere in glossy coral plastic sitting on a separate chunky black cube base that is about half the sphere's height, a ring of eight chrome buttons around the equator, a fabric grille on the front, and a visible seam running vertically over the front of the sphere. Don't render anything, just tell us what you'd change.
