PASS if the final reply summarises a design-intent package with a part list that covers at least the base, arm, shade, cable and dimmer with a material and a plausible manufacturing process for each at 5,000 units (for example injection moulding for the shade), marks numbers or choices as targets or to be confirmed, names EU compliance topics (such as electrical safety, electromagnetic compatibility or hazardous substances) as things for a specialist to confirm, lists open questions, and offers the full package as a document or a next step.

FAIL if it states that the lamp is certified, compliant or approved, presents prices or factory quotes as fact, omits materials or processes, or is a long wall of text with no summary.
